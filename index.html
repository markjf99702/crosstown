    How to play

    • Find the lines. Theme words are hidden in the map. Trace one from stop to stop in any direction, diagonals included. Drag across the letters, or tap them one at a time and tap the last letter again.
    • Every letter is used once. Each stop belongs to exactly one line, and lines never cross.
    • One word is the Crosstown. It names the theme and runs from one edge of the map to the opposite edge.
    • Detours earn fare. Any other word of four letters or more is a detour. Four letters earn 1 fare, five earn 2, six earn 3, seven or more earn 4. Every 4 fare becomes a token.
    • A token is a hint. It rings the stops of a line you haven’t found. A second token on the same line draws its route.
    • Three stars. One for finishing, one for spending no tokens, one for finding the Crosstown before any other line.
